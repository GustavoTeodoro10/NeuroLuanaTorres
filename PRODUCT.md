## Platform

web

## Stack

Fixed by the TeoCode standard (CriadorDeSites.md): plain HTML/CSS/JS plus Tailwind CSS via CDN. No build, backend, CMS or blog. Deploy target: GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

Parents and guardians of children and teenagers in Mauá and the ABC region who suspect a developmental, learning or attention difficulty and are choosing a neuropsychologist. They arrive from Google, Instagram or the AutismoPerto directory, usually on a phone, often worried, and want clarity and trust before they message anyone. The job: understand what a neuropsychological evaluation is, believe this professional is competent and warm, and start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Purpose

A first website for Luana Torres, psychologist and neuropsychologist (CRP 06/167624, neuropsychology training at Faculdade Israelita Albert Einstein), who evaluates and follows children and adolescents in Mauá, SP. The site exists to turn visits into WhatsApp conversations. Success is a parent writing to (11) 98196-3725.

## Positioning

Pediatric neuropsychological evaluation with a clear, family-friendly feedback session (devolutiva), real contact with the school and other professionals, and a care plan afterwards. Backed by 5.0 on Google across 71 reviews, which no competitor page can copy.

## Operating Context

In-person attendance at R. Álvares Machado, 281, 7º andar, sala 706, Vila Bocaina, Mauá, SP, 09310-020 (Mauá Office Center). Scheduling happens by WhatsApp. The evaluation draws on family information, clinical observation, tests and scales, school information, and ends with a devolutiva that can include referrals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer names given by the client: sessão pontual, plano 90 dias, plano 180 dias. What each includes and prices are NOT defined: never invent them.
- Weekly opening hours and e-mail were not found: omit and send visitors to WhatsApp.
- Adults are not confirmed as patients; sources say children and adolescents.
- "Atendimento para TEA" appears only in the AutismoPerto listing (public-sources, unclaimed). Use with a flag.
- Every CTA goes to WhatsApp with a contextual message and a unique `data-gc-event`. Analytics: GoatCounter only (https://teocode.goatcounter.com/count), no cookies.
- No logo exists: the name is set as text. A logo must not be created.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity before persuasion: explain the evaluation in plain words.
2. Only real proof: reviews, credentials and facts come from sources, never invented.
3. One action: talk to Luana on WhatsApp, reachable from every screen.
4. Calm and warm, not clinical and not childish: it serves worried parents.
5. Honest gaps: missing facts are omitted and reported, not filled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target: contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, landmarks, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, touch targets of at least 44px. Parents may be reading on small phones in stressful moments: short sentences, large tap areas.
